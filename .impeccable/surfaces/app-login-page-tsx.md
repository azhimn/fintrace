## Direction contract

THESIS: Each cycle is a pay envelope (amplop gaji) and each budget an envelope you spend from. It refuses the fintech default of white cards, a hero balance and a floating pill button.
OWN-WORLD: Soft pastels, pinned by the user on 2026-11-07. Pastel kraft (~#EAD3B5) owns the shell band only; faintly cool white work areas (~#F6F7FB); Record and primary actions are solid periwinkle (~#4A5BC4) with white text, because pastel fills with coloured text looked faded to the user; pastel periwinkle (~#BCC8F6) is for selected and secondary states; soft dark ink (~#3D3832) for text, never pure black. Envelope hues are pastels (mint, apricot, lilac, sky, rose, butter), one fixed hue per envelope used everywhere. Negatives and errors use a deep red (~#B3261E) for text and a pastel red for fills. Pastels are fills only: every text and figure keeps WCAG AA contrast. One workhorse sans with tabular figures carries every level, with low weight contrast (user asked on 2026-11-07): 400 body, 500 figures and labels, 600 for the few headline figures at most, never 700+. Hierarchy comes from size and spacing more than weight. Icons, only where needed: prefer Phosphor Icons (MIT, @phosphor-icons/react) in the fill weight and remove lucide-react; if Phosphor is not worth the cost (for example it fights shadcn), keep lucide-react and use filled glyphs where possible (user, 2026-11-07). Strict 4px grid. Flat and soft, modelled on a reference app the user supplied: no borders or shadows; content sits in grouped panels with a faint tinted fill, 16px corners and 1px soft dividers between rows; inputs are lighter filled boxes with ~10px corners; buttons ~14px; sheets and dialogs ~24px; the shell band stays square at the bottom and ends in a squiggly wavy flap edge (user's request; replaces the zigzag), always a whole number of waves so both ends sit at the same depth at every width (CSS background-repeat: round).
STORY: The visitor opens the app, taps Record at once, saves in seconds, and sees usable funds and envelope state update.

Then the Funds list, then Budget bars with remaining amounts. Desktop: band across the top, Record top-right, two columns below.

Signature move: the address window, a die-cut white window in kraft that frames the figure that matters (usable funds on Overview, the typed amount in Quick Record).
