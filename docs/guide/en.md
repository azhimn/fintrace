fintrace tracks your money one pay cycle at a time. Each Fund is a wallet or bank account. Each Budget is like an envelope your money is set aside in, like Meal or Fuel.

## Words used in the app

- **Cycle**: the days from one pay day to the day before the next.
- **Fund**: where money sits, like Cash, a bank or an e-wallet.
- **Budget**: what money is set aside for. New money lands in **Unallocated** until you allocate it.
- **Usable funds**: money in the Funds marked usable, minus each one's minimum balance.
- **Needs or Wants**: a label on each spend, used by Analytics.

## Start your first cycle

1. Open **Cycles** from the menu.
2. Check the start date. It is your last pay day.
3. Tap **Start cycle**.

To change which Funds count as usable, or a Fund's minimum balance, open **Settings › Funds**.

## Record a transaction

1. Tap **Record** button at the bottom right of any screen, or press `R` on a keyboard.
2. Type the item, like "Lunch". Pick a suggestion to fill in everything from last time.
3. Type the amount. Shorthand works: `50rb` or `50k` is 50.000, `1,5jt` is 1.500.000.
4. Pick the type: Expense, Income, Saving or Transfer.
5. Check **Paid from** and **Paid into**.
6. Tap **Save**.

A toast appears with **Undo** if you made a mistake. Tap **Save & add another**, or press `Ctrl` + `Enter`, to record the next one straight away.

### Optional details

Tap **More** for the date, Budget, category, quantity, discount, location, tags and note. Leave them empty and the app uses today, the category's usual Budget and quantity 1.

### Repeat a transaction fast

Under the item field, **Record again** lists recent items. Tap one, check the amount and save.

### Offline

With no connection, records are saved on your phone and sent once you are back online.

## Fill your Budgets

Income lands in **Unallocated**. Move it into Budgets so each one shows what you can still spend.

1. Open **Budgets**.
2. Tap **Allocate**.
3. Type the amount.
4. Pick the **From Budget** (usually Unallocated) and the **To Budget**.
5. Save. Undo is in the toast.

A Budget that goes below zero shows in red.

## Plan the cycle

1. Open **Plan**.
2. Tap **Add item** for each income and bill you expect, or copy the last cycle's plan and adjust it.
3. Read the running total to see what should be left at the end.

Plan items show up as suggestions when you record, so paying a planned bill takes a couple of taps.

## Fix or find a transaction

1. Open **Transactions**.
2. Filter by cycle, type, Fund, Budget, category or tag, or search the item, location or note.
3. Tap a row to edit it. Its menu also has duplicate and delete.

Transactions in a closed cycle can't be changed.

Rows without a category show as **Uncategorised** in Analytics, where you can categorise them later.

## Reconcile a Fund

Do this when the app and your real balance might differ.

1. Open **Reconcile**.
2. Count what is really in the Fund, type it under that Fund and save. For Cash, **Count notes and coins** adds the notes up for you.
3. A Fund that doesn't match shows how much is **more** or **short**.
4. Tap **Adjust** to record the difference so the app matches your count.

## Close a cycle on pay day

1. Open **Cycles**.
2. Check the **Next pay date**. Pay days can move, so change it if needed.
3. Tap **Close cycle**, then **Close it**.

Closing locks the cycle. Every Fund and Budget balance carries into the new cycle, and the plan is copied. This can't be undone.

## Borrows and Wishlist

### Borrows

Record money you lend or borrow on **Borrows**. Pick **Money out** for lending or paying back, and **Money in** for borrowing or being paid back. Each person shows what they still owe.

### Wishlist

Add things you want to buy on **Wishlist**, with a priority. When you buy one, tap **Buy**. It opens Record already filled in.

## Settings

- **General**: default pay day, theme and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Funds**, **Budgets**, **Categories**: add, rename, recolour or archive. Archived items stay in old records but leave Record and the Overview.
- **Account**: your username and password. Tap your name at the bottom of the menu.
